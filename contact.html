Attending & contact

Registration. The workshop is part of AJCAI 2026 and is open to conference registrants. Registration is handled through the main conference: ajcai26.org.

Format. Half-day, in person, at the Sir Owen G Glenn Building, 12 Grafton Road, Auckland 1010, New Zealand. Hybrid participation may be available for at most one remote invited contributor.
